Findings

  • Regeneration and fast healing are two different populations. Regeneration runs a full band higher at every level: trend ≈ 7.7 + 0.96×level, versus fast healing's ≈ 0.8 + 0.82×level. The current table was fitted to the combined pool (docs/fast-healing-scaling.md), so it under-shoots regeneration at low level and both curves inherit each other's noise.
  • Regeneration grows additively, not proportionally. The corpus mean rises ~1 point per level from a ~8-point base. Its ratio to level collapses as level grows: regen 20 at L5 is 4× level; regen 30 at L20 is 1.5× level. Ratio-preserving rescaling (scaleProportionally: value × mod(target)/mod(base)) therefore systematically overshoots any upward rescale — and explodes on spikes.
  • Trolls are exactly the spike suspected. At L3–8 the troll family carries regen 15–20 where the trend says 8–15. Paizo's own higher-level trolls answer how to scale it: Troll (L5) 20 → Troll King (L10) 30 → Jotund Troll (L15) 40 → Misbegotten Troll (L18) 45. That is +25 over 13 levels (~1.9/level) — not the ×2.5-per-9-levels our ratio math applies.
  • Values are coarse. Every printed regeneration ≥ 10 is a multiple of 5 (distinct values: 5, 7, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 40, 45, 50). Scaled output should snap to 5s.
  • There is a hard ceiling. 50 is the maximum ever printed, and 40+ appears only at level 9+ (Nightwood Guardian being the earliest). Regeneration 50 belongs to Treerazer, Mu Spore, and the Siktemporas — nothing a level-14 troop should carry.

Adopted adjustment (implemented)

Implemented in abilityScaling.ts: flat healing values scale additively along the proposed lines above, per ability kind (healingKind on the scalable value).

The rule: scale flat healing values additively along the trend, per ability kind. Replace the ratio step for healing-type flat values with a delta: new = old + (mod(target) − mod(base)), snapped to the nearest 5 (for values ≥ 10) and clamped to the corpus ceiling. This preserves a creature's spike as an offset rather than a multiplier.

Forest Troll troop check: 20 + (mod(14) − mod(5)) ≈ 20 + 9 = 29 → 30 — exactly Troll King / Mobogo territory, and one band under Troll Guard (L15, 40). Versus today's 50.

Supporting changes worth discussing:

  • Split the table: a dedicated regeneration column following the proposed line plotted above — 7 at level 0 rising to 34 at level 25 (≈ 7 + 1.08×level) — so tier buttons stop treating regen 20 at L5 as "beyond high". Fast healing's moderate column moves onto its own corpus trend (≈ 0.8 + 0.82×level), also plotted above.
  • Snap to 5s above 10, and cap at 50 (or at an observed-max envelope: ~30 through L9, 40 through L14, 45 through L17, 50 beyond).
  • Fast healing keeps its current curve — the corpus supports it — but would also move from ratio to delta scaling for consistency.
